## Summary

- **Pass**: share of queries whose check holds (right answer first, in the top 3 or top 10, or nothing found when nothing should be) with no forbidden result anywhere.
- **Hit@1**: how often the very first result is one of the best answers.
- **Recall@10**: of the right answers (best and okay), the share that shows in the first 10 results, out of as many as fit in 10.
- **MRR**: 1 ÷ the position of the first best answer, averaged (1 = always first, 0.5 = usually second, 0 = never found).
- **nDCG@10**: how close the first 10 results are to the ideal order, best answers counting double: 1 is perfect, 0 is nothing useful.
- **False positives**: of the queries that should find nothing (noise like "pizza"), the share that found something.
- **p50 / p95**: the time a query takes for half of the queries / all but the slowest 5 %.
- **Cost / 1,000**: dollars billed per thousand queries; 0 for engines that run on the device.
